FAQ

Do my customers need crypto?

No. They pay by card or UPI in their local currency. Only you, the merchant, receive crypto.

What about price swings?

By default you're paid in stablecoins (USDC/USDT) pegged to the US dollar. If you pick BTC or ETH, the quote is locked at payment time and later price moves are yours.

Is this legal in India and the US?

It can be, if it's licensed. In India, crypto service providers must register with FIU-IND and follow anti-money-laundering rules, and crypto gains carry a 30% tax plus 1% TDS on transfers. In the US, money transmitter licenses or a licensed partner are needed. The plan is to launch on top of licensed card-acquiring and on-ramp partners.

What about chargebacks?

Card payments can be disputed, but a crypto payout can't be reversed. Risk scoring, a short settlement hold for new merchants, and a reserve cover this gap.

TapRail is a concept prototype. No real payments are processed, and the rates and fees shown are illustrative.
